# Public place references

Checked 19 October 2026. This is an independent guide to Masjid Zahir in Alor Setar.

- JHEAIK records construction beginning on 11 March 1912: https://jheaik.kedah.gov.my/masjid-zahir/
- JAKIM describes five main domes representing the Five Pillars of Islam and the location opposite Balai Nobat and Istana Pelamin: https://masjid.islam.gov.my/maklumatDetailMS/1/0203026
- Kedah place-of-worship reference: https://pbt.kedah.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/15.-RUMAH-IBADAT-MASJID-ZAHIR.pdf
- Google Maps listing: https://maps.app.goo.gl/kfht5TyPhxcTxfLJ6

Maps observations are dated in the visible guide. Listing hours do not establish visitor or non-Muslim entry times, prayer times or photography permission. Admission, clothing rules and garment loans have not been confirmed; ask mosque staff.

Photo sources and licenses are recorded on each language version of the credits page.
